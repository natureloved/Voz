# Voz — Demo Script

Total runtime: ~2 minutes. No wallet needed for the demo path.

---

## Setup (before recording)

1. Open `http://localhost:3000` in a clean browser window (or production URL).
2. Have the `/demo` URL ready.
3. No wallet connection required.

---

## Script

### 0:00 — Landing page (15s)

> "This is Voz — voice-first cross-chain payments to Solana."

- Show the landing page. Point to the headline "Speak. Send. Heard."
- Let the mic animation pulse for a beat.
- Say: *"You speak. We bridge. They hear your voice."*
- Click **Try the demo**.

---

### 0:15 — Voice step (20s)

> "Imagine you're sending a birthday gift to your friend Maria in Mexico."

- The transcript types out: *"Send 25 dollars to Maria for her birthday"*
- Wait for the typing animation to finish.
- Highlight the confidence indicator: **"English · High confidence"**
- Click **Review intent**.

---

### 0:35 — Review step (15s)

> "Claude parses the who, the amount, the occasion — and resolves 'Maria' to her Solana address from the address book."

- Point to the resolved contact banner (Maria García, 7xKX…gAsU, ES).
- Point to the birthday occasion field.
- Click **Get route quote**.

---

### 0:50 — Quote step (15s)

> "LI.FI finds the best bridge from Base to Solana — Stargate in this case. Bridge fees: 8.5 cents. Gas: under a cent. Seven minutes end-to-end."

- Show the RouteCard (Stargate, fees, time).
- Show the send/receive amounts.
- Click **Execute transfer**.

---

### 1:05 — Execution timeline (20s)

> "One signature on Base. Watch the bridge happen live."

- Let the timeline animate: Approve → Bridge → Solana confirmation → Done.
- Pause on the "Bridging via Stargate" active step.
- Done checkmark animates in.

---

### 1:25 — Done + claim (35s)

> "Maria gets an email. She opens her claim link."

- Click **Open Maria's claim page**.
- The claim page loads. Show the amount ($25 / MX$435).
- Toggle to MXN to show local currency.
- The voice message auto-plays — *a natural Spanish voice reading the translated birthday message*.

> "This is Claude's translation played by ElevenLabs. Maria didn't need a wallet. She didn't need to know what USDC is. She just heard her friend's message."

- Click **Show transcript** to reveal the translated Spanish text.
- Point to the Solscan link.

---

### 1:55 — Close (5s)

> "Voz. Speak. Send. Heard."

- Hold on the claim page for a moment.
- Cut.

---

## Key moments to linger on

| Moment | Why |
|--------|-----|
| Typing transcript animation | Shows the voice input is real |
| Resolved contact banner (gold) | Shows address book integration |
| Stargate route card | Shows LI.FI integration concretely |
| Timeline animate live | Shows real bridge progression |
| Spanish voice plays on claim page | The payoff — Claude + ElevenLabs working together |
